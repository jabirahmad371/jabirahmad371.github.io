About

I am an Electrical and Electronic Engineering undergraduate at the Islamic University of Technology (IUT). My research interests lie in intelligent wireless networks, distributed communication, and learning-based methods for future cellular systems.

I approach research as a long-term process rather than a collection of quick results. I try to be disciplined about reading, implementation, simulation, validation, and revision. What motivates me most is making steady progress on problems that initially seem difficult or unfamiliar.

Research

Undergraduate Thesis · 2025–Present

Communication-efficient traffic-state estimation in highway V2V networks

I am working on decentralized gossip-based consensus using dynamic quantization and context-aware adaptation for communication-efficient traffic-state estimation in V2V networks. The work requires sustained iteration across algorithm design, simulation, and performance analysis.

Independent Research Collaboration · 2025–Present

Learning-driven gossip consensus for communication-constrained networks

I am studying learning-based gossip strategies, including multi-armed bandits and quantized communication, for distributed networks with limited communication resources. I am particularly interested in the careful process of turning an idea into a method that can be tested, compared, and improved.
