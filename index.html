About

Engineering research for communication-constrained networks.

I am an Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at the Islamic University of Technology (IUT), OIC. My research focuses on communication-efficient distributed algorithms, wireless networks, V2X systems, and learning-driven consensus methods.

I am particularly interested in combining wireless communication, optimization, reinforcement learning, and distributed intelligence to design practical methods for resource-constrained networks.

Selected Projects

Engineering projects

5G · MATLAB

5G System-Level Simulation and Performance Analysis

Analyzed user mobility, scheduling, feedback mechanisms, and link adaptation using the Vienna 5G Simulator and MATLAB.

Evaluated CQI feedback delay, throughput, effective SINR, feedback penalty, and BLER.

mmWave · NYUSIM

Wireless Channel Modeling and Analysis using NYUSIM

Modeled and analyzed millimeter-wave NLOS wireless channels under different antenna and propagation configurations.

Studied AOA, AOD, PDP, received power, delay spread, and path loss.

RF · MIMO

Microwave MIMO Antenna Design and Optimization

Designed and optimized a two-element MIMO microstrip patch antenna operating at 6.9 GHz.

Worked on impedance matching, feed optimization, DGS, S-parameters, radiation behavior, surface current, and ECC.
